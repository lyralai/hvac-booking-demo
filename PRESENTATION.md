# HVAC Lead Recovery Assistant - Presentation Script

Use this for a 7-minute HVAC company demo.

## One-Line Pitch

We recover missed HVAC jobs by answering website visitors instantly, qualifying the problem, booking the appointment, and sending your office a clean dispatch packet.

## Who This Is For

Best fit:

- owner-operated HVAC companies
- companies with a basic website but weak web conversion
- companies that miss after-hours leads
- teams that still rely heavily on phone calls and office staff
- local service companies that already use Google Calendar, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or a simple dispatch board

Poor fit:

- companies with no website traffic
- companies that cannot handle more booked jobs
- companies that require complex enterprise procurement
- teams that refuse any automation touching scheduling

## Demo Flow

### 1. Start With The Pain

Say:

> Most HVAC websites leak money after hours. A homeowner lands on the site with no heat, AC leaking, or a replacement question. If nobody answers quickly, they call the next company.

Point at:

- top metrics
- customer website preview
- owner dashboard

### 2. Run The No-Heat Scenario

Click:

- `No heat after hours`

Say:

> This is the high-value case. The homeowner needs help now. The assistant does not just chat. It gathers dispatch-grade information.

Show the assistant asking for:

- name
- phone
- service address
- booking confirmation

### 3. Point Out The Dashboard

After booking, point at:

- urgency
- booked slot
- dispatch packet
- workflow checklist
- estimated recovered revenue

Say:

> The office does not need to read a messy transcript. They get the customer, phone, address, issue, urgency, and slot.

### 4. Tie It To ROI

Say:

> If this saves one small repair, it can cover the month. If it catches one replacement quote, the month is paid many times over.

Do not overclaim exact revenue. Use "estimated value" and "recovered opportunity."

### 5. Explain The Trial

Say:

> The first version does not need to replace your whole dispatch system. We can install the widget, route leads into your current workflow, and measure booked jobs for 14 days.

## Buyer Questions And Answers

### "Will this replace our office staff?"

Answer:

> No. It handles the first response, captures details, and creates a clean handoff. Your office still owns dispatch decisions.

### "What about emergencies?"

Answer:

> Emergency policy is configurable. The assistant can escalate to your emergency line, label the lead as urgent, and still capture the details.

### "Can it connect to our calendar?"

Answer:

> Yes. The production version can read available slots from Google Calendar or route through your existing scheduling system. For the first pilot, we can start with limited controlled windows.

### "What if it says something wrong?"

Answer:

> We keep it narrow. It books service, answers approved FAQs, and escalates uncertain questions. It should not diagnose dangerous issues or make promises your company would not make.

### "How do we know it is worth it?"

Answer:

> We measure leads captured, appointments booked, after-hours leads, source, and estimated job value during the trial.

6. Embeddable script tag.

## Demo Guardrails

Say "assistant" or "booking assistant", not "fully autonomous dispatcher."

Say "estimated recovered revenue", not "guaranteed revenue."

Say "pilot" and "measure", not "set and forget."

Say "we integrate with your workflow", not "rip out your current system."
